How it works

Three moves. No fog.

Coaching here isn't a feelings circle. It's a plan, a standard, and someone who notices when you flinch.

STEP 01

Name the monster.

We map the real life underneath the busy one — the fears wearing neckties, the agreements you don't remember signing, the one domain that, fixed, changes everything else. Fog is fear's favorite habitat. We turn the lights on.

STEP 02

Build the reps.

Weekly moves you actually do — body, mind, work, money, time. Small brave over large fantasy. We win the next thirty minutes, stack proof over applause, and let momentum do what motivation never could.

STEP 03

Hold the standard.

Accountability without the shame spiral. We protect the time, fire fear as CEO, and keep raising the floor — until awesome stops being a pep talk and starts being your default setting.
