The Author's Note

This is a book about becoming awesome on purpose. Not famous. Not perfect. The kind of person who walks into a room and changes the voltage.

Too many good people — especially men — have been trained to apologize for wanting more out of life. More freedom. More money. More health. More fun. More stories worth telling when the bar gets quiet and someone finally asks, "So what did you actually do with your life?"

You can be strong without being stupid. Kind without being weak. Ambitious without becoming a soulless briefcase with blood-pressure medication. Funny, stylish, generous, fit, rich in time, rich in spirit — and hard as hell to manipulate.

It's OK to be AWESOME. Actually, it's more than OK. It's your responsibility.

— Ted Hebert

Inside the field manual

Eight chapters. Zero permission slips.

No fluff. No good-vibes wallpaper. Just the moves that turn a life built by fear, habit, and other people's low expectations into one you actually chose.

CH 01

Dare to Be Different

The first rule of an awesome life is simple: do what other people will not do. The real stuff lives on the other side of a little awkwardness.

"Small brave beats large fantasy."

CH 02

Ruthless & Aggressive Positivity

Positivity got mugged by people who use it as wallpaper. Real positivity has boundaries, teeth, and the nerve to have hard conversations.

"Carry joy like a blade."

CH 03

Eat My Shorts

Giant plans let you feel productive without doing anything. Win the next thirty minutes instead. Completion is expensive; announcements are cheap.

"Clarity creates force."

CH 04

Work Smarter, Not Harder

Hard work isn't the enemy — stupid work is. A well-paid hostage is still a hostage. Most people don't need more hustle; they need a better lever.

"Own your time or someone else rents your life."

CH 05

Fitness of Body & Mind

Your body is not a side project. It's the vehicle that carries every dream you have. Muscle is metabolic armor. Train for life.

"Convenience is stealing your pulse."

CH 06

Do What You Want, When You Want

Technology kicked a hole in the wall. The laptop is a jailbreak. Build the life that fits — you're allowed to want your life while you're still alive.

"Status wants witnesses. Freedom wants presence."

CH 07

Life on Your Terms

Don't wait to retire until you're too old to enjoy it. Later is a dangerous god. A bank account is useful — a life is the point.

"Choose awake. Later has eaten enough."

CH 08

The Only Thing to Fear

Fear is a con artist with excellent timing — it attacks motion. Name the monster and it gets smaller. Fear is allowed in the car; it does not get to drive.

"Fire fear as CEO."

The AWESOME Oath

Eight lines. Read them out loud if you've got the nerve.

  • 1I will move before I feel ready, and choose proof over applause.
  • 2I will build my body, train my mind, and protect my time.
  • 3I will use fear as a signal, not a sentence.
  • 4I will make money without selling my soul, and seek freedom without abandoning my responsibilities.
  • 5I will bring humor into heavy rooms and standards into sloppy ones.
  • 6I will not make misery my brand.
  • 7I will help others rise without shrinking to make anyone comfortable.
  • 8I will live now, love now, build now, and laugh now. I will be awesome on purpose.

Meet the author

Ted Hebert doesn't do average.

Ted writes for the people who suspect they're more capable than their current life shows — and he's right. That's not an insult. That's gasoline.

Through It's OK to be AWESOME! and the IOKTBA community, Ted helps men and women refuse the two bad deals modern life keeps offering: become a harmless houseplant, or become an angry cartoon of yourself. There's a third option — strong, kind, funny, free, and hard as hell to manipulate.

No good-vibes-only wallpaper. No permission slips. Just a better conversation about what you can do today with your body, your mind, your work, your money, your time, and your fear.
